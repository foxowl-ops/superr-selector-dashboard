Three definitions of pressure, one blended ranking

Each approach ranks every eligible player on its own. A player's rank in each is turned into a percentile, and the final score is the weighted average shown on each card.

—
Approach 1: leverage
Pressure is how far the result can swing on the next ball. A win-probability model scores every delivery, and the top quarter count as high pressure.
—
Approach 2: the scoreboard
Pressure is the death overs, or a chase needing ten or more an over. Runs and wickets inside those balls are compared with the league.
—
Approach 3: dominance
Pressure is absorbed by taking the game on from ball one: a fast start that becomes a large share of the team's runs, or of the wickets.
